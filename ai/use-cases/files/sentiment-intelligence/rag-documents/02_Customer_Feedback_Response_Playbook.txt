Customer Feedback Response Playbook
====================================

1. PURPOSE

This playbook provides standardized response templates and escalation procedures for handling customer feedback identified through AI-powered sentiment analysis. It ensures consistent, empathetic, and timely responses across all customer touchpoints.


2. RESPONSE PRINCIPLES

2.1 The HEART Framework
Every customer response should follow the HEART framework:
- Hear: Acknowledge the customer's specific experience
- Empathize: Show genuine understanding of their feelings
- Apologize: Take responsibility where appropriate (without over-apologizing)
- Resolve: Offer a concrete solution or next step
- Thank: Express gratitude for their feedback

2.2 Tone Guidelines
- Professional but warm — avoid corporate jargon
- Match the customer's energy level (don't be overly cheerful to an angry customer)
- Be specific — reference their actual complaint, not generic platitudes
- Be honest about limitations and timelines
- Never be defensive or dismissive


3. RESPONSE TEMPLATES BY SENTIMENT CATEGORY

3.1 Product Quality Complaints (Negative Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews mentioning "defective", "broke", "poor quality", "fell apart", "cheap materials"

Template:
"Thank you for sharing your experience with [Product Name]. We're sorry to hear that it didn't meet your expectations regarding [specific issue mentioned]. This is not the quality standard we aim for.

We'd like to make this right. Please contact our support team at [contact] with your order number, and we'll arrange a [replacement/refund/repair] right away. Your feedback has been shared with our product team to prevent this from happening to other customers.

We appreciate your patience and hope to earn back your trust."

Escalation: If 5+ similar complaints within 48 hours, escalate to Product Quality team for batch investigation.


3.2 Customer Service Complaints (Negative Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews mentioning "rude staff", "long wait", "no response", "unhelpful", "ignored"

Template:
"We sincerely apologize for the service experience you described. Every customer deserves to be treated with respect and to receive timely assistance — and it sounds like we fell short.

We've flagged this feedback internally and are addressing it with the team involved. We'd like to connect you with a senior support representative who can ensure your issue is fully resolved. Please reach out to [contact/link] at your convenience.

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. Feedback like yours helps us improve."

Escalation: Immediate escalation to Customer Service Manager if the review mentions a specific employee by name or describes discriminatory behavior.


3.3 Pricing Concerns (Mixed/Negative Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews mentioning "overpriced", "not worth it", "cheaper alternatives", "hidden fees"

Template:
"Thank you for your honest feedback about pricing. We understand that value for money is important, and we want you to feel confident in your purchase.

Our pricing reflects [brief value proposition — e.g., premium materials, extended warranty, free shipping]. That said, we regularly review our pricing to stay competitive. You might also want to check out our [loyalty program/seasonal sales/bundle deals] for additional savings.

We value your perspective and take pricing feedback seriously in our planning."

Escalation: If pricing complaints increase 15%+ month-over-month, escalate to Pricing Strategy team.


3.4 Delivery Issues (Negative Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews mentioning "late delivery", "damaged package", "wrong item", "lost shipment"

Template:
"We're sorry about the delivery issue you experienced. We know how frustrating it is when an order doesn't arrive as expected.

We're looking into this immediately. If you haven't already, please contact us at [contact] with your order/tracking number so we can trace the shipment and arrange a [reshipment/refund]. We're also working with our logistics partners to prevent delays like this.

Thank you for your patience — we'll make sure this gets resolved."

Escalation: If delivery complaints spike in a specific region, alert Logistics team for carrier performance review.


3.5 Positive Reviews (Positive Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews with sentiment score > 0.8, mentioning praise or recommendation

Template:
"Thank you so much for your wonderful review! We're thrilled to hear that you love [specific aspect they praised]. Feedback like yours motivates our entire team.

If you'd like to share your experience with others, we'd really appreciate a recommendation to friends or family. And don't forget to check out [related product/loyalty program] — we think you'd enjoy it!

Thanks for being a valued customer!"

Note: Positive review responses should be personalized. Avoid sending the same response to multiple reviewers on the same platform.


3.6 Feature Requests (Neutral/Positive Sentiment)

Trigger: Reviews suggesting improvements, new features, or "wish it had..."

Template:
"Thank you for the thoughtful suggestion about [specific feature]. We love hearing ideas from our customers — it helps us build better products.

Your idea has been logged with our product team for consideration in future updates. While we can't promise a timeline, customer-requested features are a priority in our development roadmap.

Keep the ideas coming, and thanks for helping us improve!"


4. ESCALATION MATRIX

4.1 When to Escalate (Beyond Standard Response)
- Product safety mentioned: IMMEDIATE escalation to Safety & Compliance
- Legal threat or litigation mention: IMMEDIATE escalation to Legal
- Media/influencer with >10K followers: Escalate to PR/Communications within 2 hours
- Viral potential (50+ engagements in 1 hour): Escalate to Crisis Communications
- Repeated issue from same customer (3+ contacts): Escalate to Customer Success Manager

4.2 Internal Communication
- Daily digest of critical and high-severity feedback to department heads
- Weekly sentiment summary to C-suite with trend analysis
- Monthly deep-dive presentation to cross-functional leadership
- Quarterly strategic review incorporating sentiment data into business planning


5. MEASURING RESPONSE EFFECTIVENESS

5.1 Key Metrics
- Response Rate: % of negative reviews that receive a response (target: >90%)
- Response Time: Average time from review detection to response (target: <4 hours for critical)
- Resolution Rate: % of complaints resolved to customer satisfaction (target: >80%)
- Sentiment Recovery: % of negative reviewers who later update their review positively
- Customer Retention: Retention rate of customers who received responses vs. those who didn't

5.2 A/B Testing Responses
- Test different response tones (formal vs. casual) per platform
- Measure impact of offering compensation vs. apology-only
- Track which response elements correlate with review updates or repeat purchases


6. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES

6.1 Amazon Reviews
- Keep responses under 500 characters
- Never ask the customer to change their review
- Focus on resolution, direct to support for order-specific issues
- Respond to all 1-2 star reviews within 24 hours

6.2 Social Media (Twitter/X, Instagram)
- Respond publicly first, then move to DM for details
- Keep initial response under 280 characters
- Use platform-appropriate tone (slightly more casual)
- Monitor for follow-up posts and respond to those too

6.3 Trustpilot / G2
- Detailed responses are expected (200-400 words)
- Reference specific points from the review
- Include your role/title for authenticity
- Invite the reviewer to update if their issue is resolved

6.4 App Store Reviews
- Very brief (1-2 sentences)
- Focus on directing to the latest app version or support channel
- Thank positive reviewers and ask for feature requests
