Brand Crisis Communication Guidelines
=======================================

1. PURPOSE

This document provides a structured framework for managing brand reputation crises detected through AI-powered sentiment monitoring. A "crisis" is defined as any event that causes a sudden, significant negative shift in brand sentiment that threatens brand reputation, customer trust, or business continuity.


2. CRISIS SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION

Level 1 — Critical Crisis (Immediate Response Required)
Indicators:
- Negative sentiment spikes >30% within 4 hours
- Product safety or health concern reported by multiple customers
- Viral social media post (>10K shares) with negative brand mention
- Major media outlet coverage of brand failure
- Data breach or privacy violation
- Regulatory action or legal proceedings made public

Response timeline: First public statement within 2 hours
Team activation: Full Crisis Response Team (CRT) including CEO, Legal, PR, Product, Customer Service

Level 2 — High Severity (Same-Day Response)
Indicators:
- Negative sentiment increase of 15-30% over 24 hours
- Cluster of similar complaints (10+) about a specific issue
- Influencer (100K+ followers) posting negative brand experience
- Competitor capitalizing on your brand weakness
- Employee misconduct gaining public attention

Response timeline: Action plan within 4 hours, public response within 8 hours
Team activation: PR Lead, Customer Service Director, relevant Department Head

Level 3 — Moderate Severity (48-Hour Response)
Indicators:
- Negative sentiment increase of 5-15% over one week
- Recurring complaint pattern emerging across multiple channels
- Negative press coverage in niche/industry publication
- Product comparison reviews trending unfavorably
- Service quality decline detected through review analysis

Response timeline: Assessment within 24 hours, action plan within 48 hours
Team activation: Customer Service Manager, Product Manager, Marketing Lead

Level 4 — Low Severity (Weekly Review Cycle)
Indicators:
- Minor negative sentiment fluctuation within normal range
- Isolated negative reviews without pattern
- Seasonal dip in satisfaction scores
- Single negative press mention without viral potential

Response timeline: Include in weekly sentiment review meeting
Team activation: Standard monitoring team


3. CRISIS RESPONSE PROTOCOL

Phase 1: Detection and Assessment (0-30 minutes)
a) AI monitoring system detects sentiment anomaly and triggers alert
b) On-call analyst reviews alert details:
   - Number and velocity of negative mentions
   - Source channels and geographic spread
   - Key phrases and topics identified
   - Influencer involvement assessment
c) Classify crisis severity level (1-4)
d) Notify appropriate stakeholders based on severity

Phase 2: Containment (30 minutes - 2 hours)
a) Assemble response team (virtual or in-person based on severity)
b) Gather all available facts — do NOT speculate
c) Prepare initial holding statement if Level 1 or 2:
   "We are aware of [issue description] and are actively investigating. Customer safety/satisfaction is our top priority. We will provide an update by [specific time]."
d) Pause any scheduled marketing campaigns that could appear tone-deaf
e) Brief customer-facing teams (support, retail, social media) with talking points
f) Monitor for escalation in real-time

Phase 3: Response (2-24 hours)
a) Issue official statement through appropriate channels:
   - Level 1: Press release + CEO social media statement + website banner
   - Level 2: Social media statement + email to affected customers + FAQ page
   - Level 3: Blog post + customer service script update + social monitoring
b) Statement must include:
   - Acknowledgment of the issue
   - What you know so far (facts only)
   - What you're doing about it
   - How affected customers can get help
   - When the next update will be provided
c) Activate customer service escalation path for related inquiries
d) Document all actions taken with timestamps

Phase 4: Resolution (24 hours - 2 weeks)
a) Implement corrective actions
b) Communicate progress updates at promised intervals
c) Provide individual resolution to affected customers
d) Monitor sentiment for recovery signals
e) Conduct root cause analysis
f) Update processes to prevent recurrence

Phase 5: Recovery (2 weeks - 3 months)
a) Launch reputation recovery campaign
b) Share improvements made as a result of the crisis
c) Engage advocates and loyal customers to amplify positive messages
d) Track sentiment recovery trajectory
e) Conduct post-crisis retrospective with all stakeholders
f) Update crisis playbook with lessons learned


4. COMMUNICATION CHANNEL STRATEGY

4.1 Social Media
- Respond to the original viral post/thread directly
- Pin an official response to your brand profile
- Monitor and respond to follow-up comments
- Do NOT delete negative comments (this always backfires)
- Do NOT get into arguments with individual commenters
- Use consistent messaging across all social platforms

4.2 Email Communication
- Proactive email to affected customers within 24 hours
- Include: What happened, what you're doing, what they should do
- Provide direct support contact (not generic support queue)
- Follow up email when resolution is achieved

4.3 Website
- Update homepage with banner for Level 1 crises
- Create dedicated FAQ page for Level 1 and 2 crises
- Ensure support contact information is prominently displayed
- Update status page if service-related

4.4 Media Relations
- Designate single spokesperson for consistency
- Prepare media brief with key messages and approved quotes
- Schedule press conference for Level 1 crises if warranted
- Provide written statements to media inquiries
- Monitor coverage and correct factual errors promptly

4.5 Internal Communication
- Brief all employees before public announcement when possible
- Provide talking points for customer-facing staff
- Update internal FAQ daily during active crisis
- Acknowledge team efforts and provide psychological support


5. DO's AND DON'Ts

DO:
- Respond quickly, even if you don't have all the answers yet
- Be transparent about what you know and don't know
- Show empathy for affected customers
- Take responsibility where appropriate
- Provide concrete next steps and timelines
- Follow through on every promise made
- Learn from the crisis and improve

DON'T:
- Ignore the crisis hoping it will go away
- Delete negative posts or reviews
- Blame customers, partners, or employees publicly
- Make promises you can't keep
- Use legal language in customer-facing communications
- Argue with critics on social media
- Launch promotional campaigns during an active crisis
- Claim the issue is isolated before investigation is complete


6. SENTIMENT RECOVERY BENCHMARKS

After a crisis, track these recovery metrics:
- Time to sentiment baseline: Average 2-6 weeks for Level 2, 1-3 months for Level 1
- Review volume normalization: Negative review spike should return to baseline within 2 weeks
- Social mention sentiment: Should show consistent positive trend within 30 days
- Customer retention rate: Compare 90-day post-crisis retention to pre-crisis baseline
- NPS recovery: Typically takes 2-4 months to reach pre-crisis levels

Red flags indicating insufficient recovery:
- Sentiment score remains 10%+ below baseline after 30 days
- Negative review keywords still appearing in new reviews
- Competitors referencing the crisis in their marketing
- Media still covering the story after initial news cycle


7. ANNUAL CRISIS PREPAREDNESS

7.1 Quarterly Activities
- Review and update crisis contact list
- Test alert notification system
- Update response templates
- Review social media monitoring keywords

7.2 Annual Activities
- Full crisis simulation exercise (tabletop or live)
- Media training for spokespersons
- Review insurance coverage for reputation incidents
- Update this crisis communication plan
- Benchmark against industry crisis response best practices
