# OCI Email Delivery Deep Dive Workshop

Reviewed: 2026-09-04

## What is this asset?

OCI Email Delivery is an Oracle Cloud Infrastructure service that enables applications and services to send high-volume, reliable transactional email using a scalable, managed SMTP or API-based platform. It is designed for emails such as password resets, account verification, notifications, receipts, and other application-generated messages.

Building and operating a reliable email infrastructure is complex due to deliverability challenges, reputation management, authentication (SPF, DKIM), scalability, and monitoring. OCI Email Delivery solves this by providing a managed, highly available email service with built-in authentication support, delivery metrics, and reputation management, allowing developers to send transactional emails securely and reliably without managing their own mail servers.
